# Rarity Mechanics

| Tier | Global Rarity Range | Meaning | Use For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 20-80% | Reachable with basic follow-through | First setup wins, simple repeatable actions |
| Uncommon | 5-20% | Requires intent and effort | Finished small projects, first public output |
| Rare | 1-5% | Requires real commitment | Reusable tools, public offers, workflow automation |
| Epic | 0.1-1% | Strong proof of capability | Client-ready work, repeat income, full systems |
| Legendary | below 0.1% | Major life or field milestone | Professional-level proof, major business outcomes |
| Mythic | below 0.01% | Elite and exceptional | Reserved for very rare long-term milestones |

## Dual Rarity Rule
- `global_rarity` asks: how rare is this among people generally?
- `industry_rarity` asks: how rare is this among people already in the relevant field?
