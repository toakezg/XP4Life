# XP Mechanics

| Tier | Quest XP | Achievement XP | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Common | 25-75 | 25-75 | Small wins and early proof |
| Uncommon | 100-200 | 100-200 | Meaningful progress and consistency |
| Rare | 250-500 | 220-350 | Strong progress with visible proof |
| Epic | 600-1200 | 500-800 | Major outcomes or capability shifts |
| Legendary | 1500+ | 1000+ | Large milestone wins |
| Mythic | custom | custom | Reserved for later |

## Pass 2 Rule
Total XP = completed quest XP + unlocked achievement XP.

Titles do not grant XP by default. They mark identity and progression.
